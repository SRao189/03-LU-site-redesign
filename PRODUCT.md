# Fran's Flowers

register: brand
mode: persuade

## What it is
Home page and About page for Fran's Flowers, a neighborhood flower shop on Valencia Street in San Francisco's Mission District. The shop is a fictional business from a web-design course brief; this redesign is portfolio work by Sahar Rao.

## Who it is for
Neighbors deciding where to buy flowers this week: they want the specials, the prices, and how to get there.

## Product truth (from the brief, do not inflate)
- Founded 1995 by Francine (Fran) Miller, Mission District.
- 10% of each sale funds efforts to fight homelessness across the Bay Area.
- Weekly specials with now/was prices; one seasonal pick.
- 123 Valencia Street, San Francisco, CA 94103; phone 123-456-7890 (placeholder from the brief).
- No hours, delivery, ordering, or reviews exist in the brief. Do not add them.

## Personality
Warm, loud, neighborly. Hand-painted shop sign, not boutique minimalism.

## Visual world
Fran's own photo: hot-pink stucco wall, teal shop door, marigold and poppy pots. Hand-painted sign lettering (Shrikhand) over a plain workhorse sans (Figtree). Price tags on string, the shop window as the specials shelf.

## Anti-references
Cream background with a serif display and sage accents (the default florist template). Card grids with icons.
