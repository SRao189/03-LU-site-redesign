About Fran

Fran Miller grew up in the Mission District and learned early that a beautiful arrangement can carry comfort, celebration, and hope in a single moment.

She opened Fran's Flowers in 1995 to create a warm neighborhood shop where people could find thoughtful blooms for birthdays, anniversaries, and everyday moments of joy.

Why She Started

Fran believes flowers should feel personal, generous, and welcoming. That belief shaped every part of the shop, from the hand-tied bouquets to the community partnerships that keep the store rooted in local care.

Today, her shop still reflects that same spirit, with each arrangement designed to brighten a room and strengthen a connection.
